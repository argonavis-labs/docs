## Built for specific roles

We don't just connect your apps and wish you luck. Runner ships with workflows, automations, and prompts designed around how people in your role actually work. The connected apps, the daily routines, the things that fall through the cracks. All of it.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Real estate agents" icon="house" href="/use-cases/real-estate-agents">
    Lead triage, listing prep, showing coordination, and follow-ups. Runner connects to Follow Up Boss, MLS, Gmail, and your calendar so you spend less time managing and more time closing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Founders" icon="rocket" href="/use-cases/founders">
    Morning briefings, inbox triage, meeting prep, investor updates, and follow-up tracking. Runner acts as your AI chief of staff across email, calendar, Slack, and your project tracker.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

More use cases coming soon. If you're using Runner for a role we haven't covered, [let us know](/support).

## How to get the most out of these pages

Each use case page includes:

- **What Runner does for your role.** The specific tasks and workflows that matter most.
- **A day in the life.** What a typical day looks like with Runner handling the busywork.
- **Prompts you can copy-paste.** Real prompts to try right now.
- **Automations to set up.** The recurring workflows that save the most time once you've got them running.

Start with whatever sounds most like your day. Then explore.
