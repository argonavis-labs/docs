## Built for specific roles

We don't just connect your apps and wish you luck. Runner ships with Workflows, Runners, and prompts designed around how people in your role actually work. The connected apps, the daily routines, the things that fall through the cracks. All of it.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Founders" icon="rocket" href="/use-cases/founders">
    Morning briefings, inbox triage, meeting prep, investor updates, and follow-up tracking. Runner acts as your AI personal assistant across email, calendar, Slack, and your project tracker.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sales & RevOps" icon="bullseye" href="/use-cases/sales-and-revops">
    Call prep, pipeline reviews, demo booking, and follow-up tracking. Runner connects to Salesforce, HubSpot, Gmail, and Calendar so you spend less time updating and more time closing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Marketing & Growth" icon="chart-line" href="/use-cases/marketing-and-growth">
    Campaign reviews, content calendars, lead attribution, and ad spend monitoring. Runner connects your analytics, CRM, and project tools.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Finance & Ops" icon="calculator" href="/use-cases/finance-and-ops">
    Board packs, invoice processing, forecast refreshes, and month-end close. Runner connects Stripe, your CRM, Sheets, and email.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Product & Engineering" icon="code" href="/use-cases/product-and-engineering">
    Sprint planning, bug triage, release monitoring, and roadmap reviews. Runner connects Linear, GitHub, Mixpanel, and PostHog.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Real estate agents" icon="house" href="/use-cases/real-estate-agents">
    Lead triage, listing prep, showing coordination, and follow-ups. Runner connects to Follow Up Boss, MLS, Gmail, and your calendar.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Personal & Family" icon="heart" href="/use-cases/personal-and-family">
    Vacation planning, subscription audits, party food, and weekend plans. Runner connects Gmail, Calendar, WhatsApp, and your browser.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

If you're using Runner for a role we haven't covered, [let us know](/support).

## How to get the most out of these pages

Each use case page includes:

- **What Runner does for your role.** The specific tasks and workflows that matter most.
- **A day in the life.** What a typical day looks like with Runner handling the busywork.
- **Prompts you can copy-paste.** Real prompts to try right now.
- **Runners to set up.** The always-on automations that save the most time once you've got them running.

Start with whatever sounds most like your day. Then explore.
