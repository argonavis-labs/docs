Runner connects to the apps you already use and does the work that eats up your day. Email, calendar, Slack, CRM, docs, browser. Instead of switching between 12 tabs and copying information from one place to another, you tell Runner what you need and it handles the rest.

That works for pretty much anyone. But we've spent a lot of time making Runner especially good at the workflows that matter most for specific roles.

## What makes Runner different from ChatGPT

You can ask ChatGPT to write you an email. But it can't check your calendar before suggesting times, look up the email thread for context, or send the reply when you're done.

Runner can. It's connected to your actual apps, so it doesn't just think about your work. It does your work.

**Concurrent tasks.** Runner runs multiple things in parallel. Draft follow-ups while pulling analytics while updating your CRM. Not one thing at a time. Everything at once.

**Browser automation.** When there's no API, Runner takes the browser wheel. It fills forms, books appointments, and navigates web apps the same way you would. Just faster.

**Memory across sessions.** Tell Runner your preferences once. How you like emails drafted, what your priorities are, who your VIPs are. It remembers and applies them every time.

**Recurring workflows.** Set a workflow to run every morning at 7:30am. Your briefing is ready before you sit down. Your inbox is triaged before your first meeting. No asking required.
