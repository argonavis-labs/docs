A lead arrives at 7:12pm. You mean to answer after dinner. By 9am they're already talking to somebody else.

The valuable work isn't "write an email." It's checking for the lead, reading the inquiry, checking your calendar, preparing the right response, and stopping once a person has replied.

That's a Runner.

<Note>
  Writes follow your [tool permissions](/connections/tool-permissions). Keep messages, CRM updates, bookings, and submissions on **Ask** while you test. Runner stages each write as an **Action Card**. Browser bookings and submissions add a final confirmation before they're submitted.
</Note>

## Start with the work closest to a transaction

| Runner | What it checks | Useful result | Review boundary |
|--------|----------------|---------------|-----------------|
| **New lead response** | Gmail, connected CRM, and Calendar | New inquiries with a reply based on the inquiry and CRM record | Review the message before it sends |
| **Lead follow-up** | Open leads and recent threads | People waiting on you, excluding anyone who replied or opted out | Keep do-not-contact rules in the source and prompt |
| **Showing prep** | Calendar, client thread, and property materials you provide | One brief with questions, instructions, and missing details | Confirm any booking or calendar write |
| **Seller update** | Showing calendar, feedback, and client email | A factual draft of activity and open next steps | Review claims before sending |
| **Weekly pipeline review** | CRM status, recent threads, and upcoming dates | Leads and clients missing an owner, reply, or next step | You choose priority and outreach |

Don't begin by automating the whole transaction. Begin with the lead that needs a reply.

## Build this first: new lead response

```text
Create a Runner named New Lead Response.

Every weekday at 8:00am and 4:00pm in my timezone, review new buyer and seller
inquiries in my connected Gmail and [CRM].

Include an inquiry only when:
- it arrived after 8am that day on the 4pm run, or after the most recent
  weekday at 4pm on the 8am run
- no agent is shown as the owner, or I am the owner
- nobody has replied after the inquiry
- the person is not marked do not contact
- no unresolved reply Action Card is already present for the inquiry

Read the full inquiry and matching CRM record. Draft a short response that
answers only what the sources support. If they asked to talk or tour, read my
Google Calendar and suggest two available windows. Don't promise availability,
pricing, property facts, representation, or a booking that isn't confirmed.

Stage the reply as an Action Card. Return the lead, inquiry time, source,
assigned owner, and proposed next step. If there are no untouched inquiries,
say that clearly and stop.

Never use or infer protected characteristics when ranking, routing, or writing
to a lead.

Before you create the Runner, show me the final name, prompt, schedule, and
timezone. Wait for my confirmation.
```

**A user-created Runner becomes active as soon as it's created.** Review its schedule and permissions, then click **Run now**.

A Runner that fires twice a day uses more of your plan. Start once a day if a second check isn't worth the cost.

Turn on **Carry unresolved Action Cards into new runs** so a pending reply can appear at the next check instead of being drafted twice. The card still expires 24 hours after it was staged.

Run it with a few test inquiries first. Read every reply. Fast and wrong isn't better than slow.

## Give it your rules

Put stable rules in a real estate Project:

- Service area, property types, and working hours.
- Lead ownership and routing rules.
- Your approved voice and response templates.
- Minimum gap between follow-ups.
- Fair housing, brokerage, and legal boundaries.
- Actions that always need you or your broker.

Keep live lead status in the CRM and conversation status in email. Each run reads those sources again. It doesn't automatically remember the prior run's output.

Start every send and CRM write on **Ask**. Keep offer, contract, booking, and submission decisions behind a person.

## Add the next check

### Lead follow-up

Run every weekday morning. Find open leads with no reply or next step after the gap you set. Read the actual thread before drafting anything. Stop when the source says the person replied, opted out, or the lead closed.

### Showing prep

Run each morning. For today's showings, gather the client thread, calendar details, and property documents you provided. List missing instructions instead of filling the gaps with guesses.

### Seller update

Run every weekday at 5pm. If Calendar shows no showing that day, stop. Otherwise, draft a short update from confirmed activity, feedback, and open questions. Don't invent buyer interest or market signals.

### Weekly pipeline review

Run Friday afternoon. Pull current CRM status, recent client threads, and next week's dates into one list. Flag missing owners and next steps. Don't rank people by vague labels such as "hot" unless your CRM contains an explicit rule.

## Speed matters. So does trust.

The first win is simple: new leads get checked on schedule, the reply uses real context, and nothing sends before you're ready.

Then you add the next Runner.
