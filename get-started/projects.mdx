You'll end up with a lot of sessions. Board prep, a hiring push, that trip you're planning, the newsletter. Projects keep them organized by the thing you're working on, with your sessions and your Runners grouped together in one place.

If you used workspaces or labels on Runner Desktop, Projects replace both. They're more powerful than either.

## What a Project holds

Each Project groups three things:

- **Sessions.** Every conversation related to this work lives under the Project in your sidebar.
- **Runners.** Always-on automations can belong to a Project too. Your "Weekly pipeline review" Runner lives inside your Sales project, right next to the sessions it creates.
- **Context.** This is the part that makes Projects powerful. Keep reading.

---

## Project context

Why are Projects better than folders? One field: **Project context**.

Open any Project, click edit, and you'll find a place to write shared context Runner should use for every task in that Project. Who the client is. What the goal is. What "done" looks like. Links that matter.

Write it once. Every session and every Runner in that Project starts already knowing it. No more pasting the same background into every new session.

> **Project context for "Q3 Fundraise":** We're raising a $5M seed. Target close is October 15th. Lead conversations are with Haun and Founders Fund. Never email investors directly, always draft for my review. Data room lives in Google Drive under /Fundraise.

## Create a Project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New project">
    In the sidebar, click **New project** and give it a short, clear name. "Q3 Fundraise". "House Move". "Newsletter".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the context">
    Open the Project and edit its details. Fill in the **Project context** field with what Runner should know for everything inside it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start working">
    Use the **Start a new session** box right on the Project page. Or drag existing sessions and Runners in from the sidebar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Good to know

- **Sessions and Runners can move.** Drag them between Projects in the sidebar. A Runner that doesn't belong anywhere just lives outside of Projects, which is fine.
- **Recent catches strays.** Sessions you haven't filed show up in the **Recent** section of your sidebar.
- **Projects sync everywhere.** Same Projects on your laptop, phone, and iPad. [Add Runner to your home screen →](/get-started/runner-on-your-phone)
