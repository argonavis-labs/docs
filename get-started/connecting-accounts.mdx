## Connect the tools for the first job

| AI teammate | Start with |
|-------------|------------|
| **Personal Assistant** | Gmail and Google Calendar |
| **Pipeline Inspector** | HubSpot or Salesforce, plus Gmail |
| **Invoice Check** | Gmail or Drive, Google Sheets, and the agreement folder |
| **Customer-impact check** | Mixpanel, Linear, and GitHub |

Add more only when the job needs them.

## How it works

When you connect an app, Runner opens the provider's sign-in and permission screen. Review the access it requests, choose an account if prompted, and approve it. You can then set a permission for each tool on that account.

<Info>
  Security and policy details change. The current versions live on Runner's [Security](https://runner.now/security), [Privacy Policy](https://runner.now/privacy), and [Terms of Service](https://runner.now/terms) pages.
</Info>

## Connect your first app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connected Apps">
    In Runner, click **Connected Apps** in the sidebar.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect an app">
    Search by name or find the app under **Available**, then click **Connect**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the account">
    If this is your first connection, review the notice and click **Connect**. Finish the provider's sign-in steps in the window that opens.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check its tools">
    Once the account appears under **Connected**, open it and review **Tools**. Start read tools on **On** and write tools on **Ask**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it">
    Ask for something small:

    - *"What's on my calendar today?"*
    - *"Show me my unread emails"*
    - *"Draft a message to #general on Slack. Don't send it yet."*
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Work and personal, both connected

Have two Gmail accounts? Open **Connected Apps**, find Gmail under **Available**, and click **Add account**. Runner lists each account separately, and you can tell it which one to use: *"Reply from my work email."*

To keep the work organized, use [Projects](/get-started/projects). A Project groups related sessions and Runners and gives them shared context.

---

## You stay in control

Every connected account exposes its tools, and you decide what each one can do: **On**, **Allow**, **Ask**, or **Off**.

Set email search to **On** and sending to **Ask**, for example. Runner can search freely. A send waits in an **Action Card** until you approve it.

[How tool permissions work →](/connections/tool-permissions)

## Public share links

When you choose **Share online** for a session or file, Runner creates a public link. Anyone with the link can view it until you choose **Stop sharing**.

Open that session or file's share menu and click **Stop sharing** when you no longer want the link to work.

---

## Reconnect or disconnect

**If a connection needs attention**, click **Reconnect** beside the account and finish the provider's sign-in steps.

**To remove an account**, open its actions in **Connected Apps** and click **Disconnect**. After Runner confirms the disconnect, that account is no longer offered to Runner.

If Runner shows **Access may still be active**, you must also revoke Runner in the provider's account or security settings.
