---
title: "Runner on your phone"
description: "Add Runner to your home screen and take your assistant everywhere."
---

Getting Runner onto every device was one of the main reasons we moved to the web. There's no app store download. You add [app.runner.now](https://app.runner.now) to your home screen and it behaves like a native app: its own icon, full screen, one tap away.

Same sessions, same files, same memories as your laptop. Dictate a task while you're walking to a meeting, close your phone, and check the result when you sit down.

## iPhone and iPad

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Runner in your browser">
    Go to [app.runner.now](https://app.runner.now) in Safari or Chrome and sign in. Everything you set up on your laptop is already there.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/mobile/add-to-home-1.png" alt="Typing app.runner.now into the browser on iPhone" width="320" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the share menu">
    Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow) in the address bar.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/mobile/add-to-home-2.png" alt="Runner open in Safari with the Share button highlighted" width="320" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Home Screen">
    Scroll down the share menu until you see **Add to Home Screen**. If you don't see it, tap **View More** first.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/mobile/add-to-home-3.png" alt="The share menu with Add to Home Screen highlighted" width="320" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and add">
    Make sure **Open as Web App** is toggled on, then tap **Add**. Runner lands on your home screen like any other app.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/mobile/add-to-home-4.png" alt="The Add to Home Screen dialog with Open as Web App toggled on" width="320" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Android

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Runner in Chrome">
    Go to [app.runner.now](https://app.runner.now) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Tap the **three-dot menu** in the top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Home screen">
    Tap **Add to Home screen** (on some phones it says **Install app**), then confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  This works on anything with a browser: Chromebooks, tablets, your partner's iPad. Sign in, add to home screen, done.
</Tip>

---

## What people actually use it for

- **Voice notes on the go.** Tap the mic, describe what you need, board your flight. Runner works while you're in the air.
- **Reviewing Action Cards.** When a write tool is set to **Ask**, Runner stages an **Action Card** you can review from your phone. **Allow** performs the action without a card. **Off** blocks the tool unless a user-created Runner has its own override.
- **Reading your Daily Brief.** Once you turn its schedule on, each 8am run is available on your phone.
