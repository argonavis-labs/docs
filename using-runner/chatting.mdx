Runner isn't a chatbot. It's connected to your actual apps and it takes real action. You tell it what you need, it does the rest.

<Note>
  Writes follow your [tool permissions](/connections/tool-permissions). **Ask** stages an **Action Card** for review. **Allow** acts without asking. **Off** blocks the tool unless a user-created Runner has its own override.
</Note>

## You talk, Runner runs

Other AI tools give you advice and ask you to take the next step yourself. Runner does the work.

| You say | Runner does |
|---|---|
| *"Triage my inbox"* | Reads your last 7 days of email. Classifies everything by urgency. Drafts replies for what matters. Archives the noise. |
| *"Prep me for my meetings tomorrow"* | Pulls your calendar, researches every attendee, finds related email threads, builds a one-page brief for each meeting. |
| *"Reply to Jordan's email with available times this week"* | Reads the thread, checks your calendar for open slots, drafts a reply with your availability. |
| *"Find Series A fintech companies in the Bay Area and reach their VP of Sales"* | Searches for matching companies, identifies decision-makers, gets contact info, drafts personalized outreach for each one. |

No special syntax. No commands. Just say it like you'd say it to a capable colleague.

## Save what should carry forward

Want a preference available in future sessions? Ask Runner to remember it:

- *"When you draft emails for me, keep them short. Three sentences max."*
- *"Always check my calendar before suggesting meeting times."*
- *"Emails from Sarah and James are always high priority."*

Then open **Memory** and make sure it was saved. Future sessions can use saved facts and preferences. The transcript itself doesn't become Memory. [See and edit Personal Memory →](/using-runner/memory)

## Voice and files

**Voice input.** Click the mic icon to talk instead of type. Dictate a task from your phone on the way to the gate, close it, and check the result when you land. [Runner on your phone →](/get-started/runner-on-your-phone)

**Attach files.** Drag a PDF, spreadsheet, or image into the conversation. *"Review this proposal and give me the three biggest concerns."*

---

## Sessions follow you

Every session syncs across your devices. Start on your laptop, continue on your phone.

Need context from another session? Ask Runner to read another active session you own and use it here. It can't send a message into or resume that other session.

---

## Tips

- **Be specific.** *"Check the email from Sarah about the Q2 budget"* beats *"check my email."*
- **Chain tasks.** *"Check my calendar, prep for my meetings, and draft a Slack standup."* One message, three jobs.
- **Correct it.** If Runner does something unexpected, tell it what you wanted instead. If the correction should stick, save it to **Memory**.
