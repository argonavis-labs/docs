A session is where you work with Runner. Brief the job, answer questions, check the evidence, make the call, and improve the result.

Runner can find, compare, organize, and draft across the sources you give it. You keep the judgment, relationships, creativity, and leadership that need a person.

<Note>
  Writes follow your [tool permissions](/connections/tool-permissions). **Ask** stages an **Action Card** for review. **Allow** acts without asking. **Off** blocks the tool unless a user-created Runner has its own override.
</Note>

## Work together in a session

| You ask | Runner handles | You decide |
|---------|----------------|------------|
| *"Triage email from the last seven days"* | Reads, groups, cites, and drafts from the inbox | What matters, how a reply should land, and whether to send |
| *"Prep me for tomorrow's customer meeting"* | Pulls the calendar, relevant threads, open commitments, and named Project sources | The goal, questions, relationship, and how to lead the room |
| *"Check proposals waiting on a reply"* | Finds the gaps, reads the thread, and drafts the next step | Timing, price, negotiation, and the final message |
| *"Compare these invoices with the signed terms"* | Shows mismatches and links the evidence | Whether it's correct, how to handle the vendor, and whether to pay |

No special syntax. Say it like you'd say it to a capable colleague, then make the decisions that need you.

## Save what should carry forward

Want a preference available in future sessions? Ask Runner to remember it:

- *"When you draft emails for me, keep them short. Three sentences max."*
- *"Always check my calendar before suggesting meeting times."*
- *"Emails from Sarah and James are always high priority."*

Then open **Memory** and make sure it was saved. Future sessions can use saved facts and preferences. The transcript itself doesn't become Memory. [See and edit Personal Memory →](/using-runner/memory)

## Voice and files

**Voice input.** Click the mic icon to talk instead of type. Dictate a task from your phone on the way to the gate, close it, and check the result when you land. [Runner on your phone →](/get-started/runner-on-your-phone)

**Attach files.** Drag a PDF, spreadsheet, or image into the conversation. *"Review this proposal and give me the three biggest concerns."*

## Sessions follow you

Every session syncs across your devices. Start on your laptop, continue on your phone.

Need context from another session? Ask Runner to read another active session you own and use it here. It can't send a message into or resume that other session.

## Give Runner a clear brief

- **Give one clear outcome.** *"Check the email from Sarah about the Q2 budget"* beats *"check my email."*
- **Name the sources.** Say which inbox, Project, folder, record, or site holds the truth.
- **Set the review point.** Say what should stay a draft. Use **Ask** or **Off** for hard action boundaries.
- **Correct the work.** Tell Runner what was wrong and what good looks like.

Feedback in one session doesn't rewrite a scheduled Runner. For a lasting job change, update its saved prompt. Put durable personal facts and preferences in Personal Memory.

## When to use a session, Workflow, or Runner

- Use a **session** to work through something once.
- Save a **Workflow** when the procedure is useful again and you want to start it with `/slug`.
- Create a **Runner** when one AI employee should own the job on a schedule.

Test the work in a session first. Save the procedure as a Workflow when you want it again. Create a Runner when the result is useful, repeatable, and ready for a schedule.
