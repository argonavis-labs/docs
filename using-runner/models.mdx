Runner v1 ran on Claude. Runner v2 runs on whatever model is right for the job.

Claude, OpenAI, Grok, Gemini, and strong open-source models like Kimi K3, MiniMax M3, GLM 5.2, and DeepSeek V4. All in one app, with your context, your memory, and your connected apps behind every one of them.

## Start with intelligence, not models

You don't need to know the model landscape. Runner gives you three intelligence settings:

| Setting | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Auto** | Runner chooses the model for you, per task. The right default for almost everyone. |
| **Normal** | Good for everyday tasks. Efficient on usage. |
| **Ultra** | More usage, better on hard tasks. Deep research, long documents, gnarly reasoning. |

Leave it on Auto. Bump a session or a Runner to Ultra when the task is genuinely hard. That's 95% of what you need to know about models.

In **Settings, then Behavior**, you can choose which model sits behind Normal and which behind Ultra.

---

## Or pick the exact model

Want control? Open the model picker in any session and choose from the full catalog: Claude Sonnet and Opus, GPT-5.6, Grok, Gemini, Kimi K3, MiniMax M3, GLM 5.2, DeepSeek V4, and more. Runners have their own intelligence setting too, so your heavyweight research Runner and your lightweight inbox Runner can each use what fits.

The catalog grows as new models ship. Whatever is current is in the picker.

---

## How usage works

Different models cost very different amounts to run, and your usage reflects that.

Each model in the picker shows its usage relative to the baseline. Frontier models like Claude Opus are the expensive end. Open-source models like GLM 5.2 and MiniMax M3 run at a fraction of that. Pricing tracks what the models actually cost, so when providers drop prices, your usage goes further.

Why does the difference matter? Simply put, a Runner that fires every hour on an expensive model adds up. Match the model to the job:

- **Daily briefs, triage, summaries:** Normal, or an efficient open-source model. These tasks don't need a frontier model.
- **Research, analysis, anything you'd call "hard":** Ultra. The extra usage buys real quality.
- **Everything else:** Auto. Let Runner make this call per task.
