## Get running in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Runner">
    Download the desktop app from [runner.now](https://runner.now) and open it. Works on Mac, Windows, and Linux.

    [Full installation guide →](/get-started/installation)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in with your Google account. You'll land in your first workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect an app">
    Open **Connected Apps** and connect your first app. Most people start here:

    <CardGroup cols={3}>
      <Card icon={<img src="/images/apps/gmail.svg" alt="Gmail" width="24" height="24" />} title="Gmail" />
      <Card icon={<img src="/images/apps/google-calendar.svg" alt="Calendar" width="24" height="24" />} title="Calendar" />
      <Card icon={<img src="/images/apps/slack.svg" alt="Slack" width="24" height="24" />} title="Slack" />
    </CardGroup>

    One click, sign in, done. [Full guide to connecting apps →](/get-started/connecting-accounts)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask Runner something">
    Start a conversation and try one of these:

    - *"What's on my calendar today?"*
    - *"Summarize my unread emails"*
    - *"Draft a reply to the last email from [name]"*

    Runner reads your connected apps, does the work, and shows you the result. Emails come back as draft cards you can edit and send. Calendar queries show your real schedule. Everything is live, not made up.

    <Tip>
      Try *"Give me my morning briefing"* for the full experience. Runner checks your calendar, triages your inbox, and tells you what needs your attention right now. It's the single best way to see what Runner can do.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Your first week

You don't need to learn everything at once. Here's a good progression:

1. **Day 1:** Connect Gmail and Calendar. Ask for your morning briefing.
2. **Day 2:** Try inbox triage. Let Runner classify your emails and draft replies.
3. **Day 3:** Connect Slack. Ask Runner what you missed overnight.
4. **Day 4:** Set up your first automation (morning briefing at 8am is a great start).
5. **Day 5:** Connect your project tracker (Linear, Notion, etc.). Ask for a weekly review.

By the end of the week, Runner handles most of the small, predictable work that eats your day.
