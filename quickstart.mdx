By the end of this page you'll have your own Personal Assistant. A Runner that knows your calendar, tracks your commitments, and shows up every morning at 9am with something useful to say.

No download. No install. About 15 minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in at app.runner.now">
    Open any browser on any device and go to [app.runner.now](https://app.runner.now). Sign in with your Google account.

    That's the whole install. Runner runs on a dedicated virtual computer in the cloud, so it works the same from your laptop, your phone, or your iPad. [Put it on your home screen →](/get-started/runner-on-your-phone)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Gmail and Google Calendar">
    Open **Connected Apps** and connect both:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card icon={<img src="/images/apps/gmail.svg" alt="Gmail" width="24" height="24" />} title="Gmail">
        One click, sign in with Google. Takes 30 seconds.
      </Card>
      <Card icon={<img src="/images/apps/google-calendar.svg" alt="Google Calendar" width="24" height="24" />} title="Google Calendar">
        Same Google account. Runner sees your inbox and your schedule together.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    That's all you need to start. [See all apps you can connect →](/get-started/connecting-accounts)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet your built-in Runners">
    Head to the **Runners** tab. Two are already waiting for you:

    - **Heartbeat** monitors your connected communication channels and suggests what to do next, twice a day on weekdays.
    - **Daily Brief** gives you a full update of everything you need to know, every day at 8am in your timezone.

    Both start paused so you stay in control. Open each one and hit **Enable**.

    These run 24/7 in the cloud whether your laptop is open or not. For the cost of a Runner subscription, you have an always-on computer with your secure personal context that can take approved actions on your behalf.

    [More on Heartbeat and Daily Brief →](/runners/heartbeat-and-daily-brief)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your Personal Assistant">
    Now the fun part. You're going to create your master Runner. Something you can talk to about your work, your priorities, and everything on your plate.

    In the **Runners** tab, click **New runner** in the top right corner, then select **Ask Runner to create it**. This opens a new session with a pre-filled prompt. Add these lines at the bottom:

    ```text
    I want you to become my personal assistant. I want you to look at my
    communications, my calendar, my commitments and give me
    suggestions on more things that I could automate. I want you to review
    Heartbeat and my Daily Brief and keep me organized. I want you to help me
    save time and be more efficient so I can spend more time doing the things
    I love in life. Keep track of my open loops, the things that I say yes to,
    and be as proactive as possible. Finally, I want you to run every morning
    at 9:00am and give me proactive tips for how best to use Runner each day.
    ```

    Hit **Send**, then have a conversation with Runner until it has what it needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize and run it">
    Click on your new Runner in the Runners tab to open its details. From here you can:

    - Turn it on and off
    - Review the full prompt Runner generated on your behalf
    - Decide if it suggests **Action Cards**, previews of actions you can approve with one click (keep this on for now)
    - Scroll down and set an avatar to give your Personal Assistant some personality

    Hit **Save changes**, scroll back to the top, and hit **Run now**. It appears at the top of your recent sessions and gets to work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it feedback">
    Your new Personal Assistant will have questions and things for you to review. Sit and chat with it for a few minutes, and remember the one thing that separates OK users from power users: feedback.

    Give feedback the way you would to a colleague or friend:

    - *"I didn't like that example, can you be more specific."*
    - *"You recommended that I drive there, but I don't have a car. Please remember to use transit options next time."*
    - *"I want to get notified of key events at least 2 days before they happen, not the morning of."*

    Runner remembers, and every session gets better than the last.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That's it. You now have an AI Personal Assistant that activates every morning with helpful things to guide you to a better day. Keep giving it feedback, keep asking it to do more, and hold it to your high standards.

## What to try next

Your Personal Assistant is the start, not the end. Type any of these into a new session:

| Say this | What happens |
|---|---|
| *"Triage my inbox"* | Runner classifies every email, drafts replies for what needs a response, archives the noise |
| *"Prep me for my next meeting"* | Runner researches attendees, pulls relevant emails, builds a one-page brief |
| *"Draft a reply to the last email from [name]"* | Runner reads the thread, checks your calendar, drafts a response you can review |
| *"What emails did I send this week with no reply?"* | Runner checks your sent folder and flags what's gone stale |

---

## Your first week

You don't need to learn everything at once. Here's a good progression:

1. **Day 1:** Sign in, connect Gmail and Calendar, build your Personal Assistant.
2. **Day 2:** Read your Daily Brief. Tell it what you want more and less of.
3. **Day 3:** Connect Slack. Ask Runner what you missed overnight.
4. **Day 4:** [Add Runner to your phone](/get-started/runner-on-your-phone). Dictate a task on the go.
5. **Day 5:** Create a second Runner for something specific: a 5am morning brief delivered to your inbox, a Friday weekly review, a follow-up tracker.

By the end of the week, Runner handles most of the small, predictable work that eats your day.
