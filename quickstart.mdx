## Get running in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Runner">
    Download the desktop app from [runner.now](https://runner.now) and open it. Works on Mac, Windows, and Linux.

    [Full installation guide →](/get-started/installation)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in with your Google account. You'll land in your first workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect an app">
    Open **Connected Apps** and connect your first app. Most people start here:

    <CardGroup cols={3}>
      <Card icon={<img src="/images/apps/gmail.svg" alt="Gmail" width="24" height="24" />} title="Gmail" />
      <Card icon={<img src="/images/apps/google-calendar.svg" alt="Calendar" width="24" height="24" />} title="Calendar" />
      <Card icon={<img src="/images/apps/slack.svg" alt="Slack" width="24" height="24" />} title="Slack" />
    </CardGroup>

    One click, sign in, done. [Full guide to connecting apps →](/get-started/connecting-accounts)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask Runner something">
    Start a conversation and try one of these:

    - *"What's on my calendar today?"*
    - *"Summarize my unread emails"*
    - *"Draft a reply to the last email from [name]"*

    Runner reads your connected apps, does the work, and shows you the result.
  </Step>
</Steps>
