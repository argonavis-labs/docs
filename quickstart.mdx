Build your first AI employee: a Personal Assistant for your 9am attention brief.

It reads Gmail and Google Calendar, spots what needs attention, and gives you a plan. You set the priorities, review sensitive replies, and make final decisions.

| Runner | Sources | Result | You decide |
|--------|---------|--------|------------|
| **Personal Assistant** | Gmail and Google Calendar | A short plan every morning at 9am | Priorities, sensitive replies, and final decisions |

You'll connect two apps, check their permissions, create the Runner, then run it once.

<Note>
  The screenshots show mine, named Shiela: Kent's Chief of Staff. That's the display name. In this guide, her only job is the 9am attention brief. Give yours any name you want.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Runner">
    Go to [Runner](https://app.runner.now/app) in any browser and sign in. There's nothing to install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Gmail and Google Calendar">
    Open **Connected Apps** and connect Gmail and Google Calendar.

    Open **Tools** on each account. Set read tools to **On** and write tools to **Ask**. **On** lets Runner read. **Ask** stages an **Action Card** for review. **Allow** acts without asking. **Off** blocks the tool unless a user-created Runner has its own override.

    [Connect your apps →](/get-started/connecting-accounts)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the Runner">
    Open **Runners** in the sidebar. You'll see **Heartbeat** and **Daily Brief** there too. Both ship paused, so leave them that way for now.

    Click **New runner**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/quickstart/new-runner.png" alt="The New runner button on the Runners page" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask Runner to set up the job">
    Choose **Ask Runner to create it**. This opens a new session with the first line already filled in.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/quickstart/ask-runner-to-create.png" alt="An arrow points to Ask Runner to create it in the Create a runner window" />
    </Frame>

    Set the session intelligence to **Normal** before you continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your Personal Assistant">
    Add this below the prefilled line:

    ```text
    Create a Personal Assistant Runner for me.

    Every day at 9:00am in my timezone, review my connected Gmail and Google
    Calendar. Give me a short plan for the day with:

    1. Three items that may deserve my attention, with the source and why.
    2. Meetings that need preparation.
    3. Commitments and open loops that need attention.
    4. Schedule conflicts, deadlines, or important messages I may have missed.

    Be specific. If the source is unclear, say so instead of guessing. If a
    write action would help, stage it as an Action Card and wait for my approval.

    Don't add Runner-specific tool permission overrides. My write tools are
    set to Ask in Connected Apps.

    Ask me whatever you need. Before you create the Runner, show me the final
    name, prompt, schedule, and timezone. Wait for my confirmation.
    ```

    Click **Send**, then answer Runner's questions. Give it real details. One current priority and one thing it shouldn't touch will do more than another paragraph about being proactive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the setup">
    Read the final name and prompt. Check the 9am schedule and timezone. Because you started from the main **Runners** page, it begins under **No project** with no Runner-specific tool permission overrides.

    **A user-created Runner becomes active as soon as it's created.** Confirm only when you're ready for the schedule to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the job description">
    Open **Runners** and select your new Personal Assistant.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/quickstart/personal-assistant-settings.png" alt="Shiela: Kent's Chief of Staff showing the Status and Action Cards controls" />
    </Frame>

    Treat the saved prompt as the job description. Check the prompt, schedule, timezone, **Project**, and **Runner model**. Choose a Project if you want its context. Keep the model on **Normal** for now. Scheduled Runners don't use **Auto**. **Ultra** is for harder jobs and uses more of your plan.

    Under **Tool permissions**, leave **No overrides** while you test. The Runner uses your current **Connected Apps** settings. If you add an override later, it takes priority, including over **Off**.

    The **Action Cards** switch carries unresolved cards into later runs. It doesn't carry old run output, and a pending card still expires after 24 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a face">
    Pick an avatar. It saves immediately. If you changed anything else, click **Save changes**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/quickstart/choose-an-avatar.png" alt="The avatar picker and Save changes button on a Runner" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it now">
    Don't wait until tomorrow morning. Click **Run now**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/quickstart/run-now.png" alt="An arrow points to Run now on Shiela: Kent's Chief of Staff" />
    </Frame>

    The test creates a separate session and keeps you on the Runner detail page. You can run a paused Runner this way too.

    Open **Recent runs** and click the run's timestamp. You can also click your Personal Assistant's existing row under **Recent** in the sidebar.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/quickstart/open-recent-session.png" alt="An arrow points to Shiela: Kent's Chief of Staff under Recent" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it useful feedback">
    Talk to it the way you would a colleague. Be direct:

    - *"I didn't like that example. Be more specific."*
    - *"I don't have a car. Use transit options next time."*
    - *"Tell me about key events at least two days before they happen, not the morning of."*

    If the feedback should apply every morning, add it to the saved **Prompt**. Put durable facts and preferences in [Personal Memory](/using-runner/memory).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What carries into the next run

Each run starts in a separate session. Your saved prompt, schedule, timezone, Project, and intelligence setting stay with the Runner. So do any Runner-specific tool permission overrides. Tools without an override use your current **Connected Apps** settings.

The previous run's output doesn't come along automatically. Put standing instructions in the saved prompt, background in Project context, and durable personal details in Personal Memory.
