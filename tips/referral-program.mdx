---
title: "Refer a friend"
description: "Invite someone to Runner. After they pay, you each get $50 in Runner credits."
---

Share your invite link with someone who's new to Runner. With the standard offer, they get $50 in Runner credits after they pay for Runner. You get $50 too.

<Note>
  **Settings**, then **Referrals** shows the current reward and invite limit for your account. The standard limit is 200 referrals.
</Note>

## Send your invite link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Referrals">
    Click **Settings**, then **Referrals**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your link">
    Under **Your invite link**, click **Copy**. Send the full link by text, email, or Slack.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They create an account">
    They open the link, click **Accept invite**, and sign up for Runner. The link carries your referral code, so there's nothing for them to type during signup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They pay for Runner">
    Runner adds $50 in credits to each account after the referred user pays. Signup alone records a pending referral. It doesn't issue the credits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Two time windows matter

When someone opens your invite link, Runner remembers it in that browser for up to 90 days. They should finish signup in the same browser so Runner can match the new account to your link. If they open a newer Runner invite link, that link replaces the earlier one.

Runner can only attach a referral during the first 24 hours after the account is created. An older account can't claim one.

Why two windows? The 90-day window remembers which link they opened. The 24-hour window makes sure the reward goes to a new Runner account.

## Track pending and paid referrals

Open **Settings**, then **Referrals**, and look under **Your invites**:

- **Invited** means Runner recorded the signup and the referral is pending.
- **Converted** means the referred user paid and the referral credits were added.

The count above the list shows how many referrals you've used and your current limit. The standard limit is 200.

## Customize your code

Your referral code is the last part of your invite link, such as `ada2026`. In **Customize your code**, you can choose a code or generate a new one.

Codes must use 4 to 10 lowercase letters and numbers. No spaces or punctuation.

**Changing your code disables the old invite link.** Copy the new link before you share again. Referrals already recorded with the old code stay in your history.

## Your accounts stay separate

You can see the referred user's signup email, referral dates, and status under **Your invites**. You don't get access to their Runner content.

A referral doesn't connect your Runner accounts. You can't see each other's sessions, Runners, connected apps, or other Runner activity.
