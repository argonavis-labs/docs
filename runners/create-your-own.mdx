There are two ways to create a Runner. One is filling out a form. The other is telling Runner what you want and letting it build the thing for you.

Use the second one.

## Ask Runner to create it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from the Runners tab">
    Click **New runner** in the top right corner, then choose **Ask Runner to create it**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what you want">
    A session opens with a pre-filled prompt. Add what you actually want in plain language:

    > *"Every Friday at 4pm, review my week across calendar and email. What did I spend time on, what slipped, what's still open. Email me the summary."*

    Runner asks follow-up questions, then creates the Runner for you: prompt, schedule, everything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and enable">
    Open your new Runner from the Runners tab. Read the prompt it generated on your behalf, adjust anything, hit **Save changes**, and make sure it's **Active**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it once, right now">
    Don't wait for the schedule. Hit **Run now** and watch the first run land in your recent sessions. Then give it feedback like you would a colleague. The second run is always better than the first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Prefer the form? **New runner** also lets you fill in the name, prompt, schedule, timezone, Project, and intelligence level yourself. Same result, more typing.
</Tip>

## The settings that matter

Once a Runner exists, its details page gives you the controls:

- **Active or Paused.** Paused Runners keep their prompt and history, they just don't fire. Pause instead of delete.
- **Schedule.** Daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly, or custom cron. The page shows you in plain English when it will run next.
- **Tool permissions.** Decide per tool what this Runner can do on its own: read your inbox, yes. Send email without asking, your call. [How permissions work →](/connections/tool-permissions)
- **Action Cards.** Keep "carry unresolved Action Cards into new runs" on so a draft you didn't get to today shows up again tomorrow.
- **Intelligence.** Auto is right for most Runners. Bump a hard one (deep research, long documents) to Ultra. [More on models →](/using-runner/models)
- **Avatar.** Give it a face. You'll be working with it every day.

## Runner ideas that earn their keep

| Runner | Schedule | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Morning brief to inbox | Daily, 5am | Emails you the day's brief before you wake up |
| Follow-up tracker | Weekdays, 5pm | Flags sent emails with no reply and drafts nudges |
| Weekly review | Fridays, 4pm | Where your week went, what slipped, what's next |
| Pipeline watch | Weekdays, 8am | Reviews CRM changes and flags deals going quiet |
| Competitor watch | Mondays, 9am | Checks competitor sites and news, reports what changed |
| Inbox zero on messages | Daily, noon | Drafts replies to unanswered WhatsApp and Slack messages |

Start with one. Trust it, then add the next.
