Every Runner account ships with two built-in Runners. They start paused so you decide when the always-on era begins. Open the **Runners** tab, click into each one, and hit **Enable**.

## Daily Brief

Every day at 8am in your timezone, Daily Brief reads your calendar, your inbox, and your connected apps, and writes you the update a great personal assistant would: what needs you today, what's coming, what you're waiting on.

It's built to be read in about 90 seconds. Not a wall of text. A brief.

Want it in your inbox instead of the app? Tell it. The stock prompt already asks Runner to notify you by email when the brief is ready, and you control email limits in **Settings, then Notifications**.

**Make it yours.** Daily Brief is a Runner like any other, so you can edit everything:

- Move it to 6am. Or 5am with an email, so it's waiting when you wake up.
- Tell it what matters: *"Always lead with anything from investors. Never include newsletter summaries."*
- Change its tone. Some people want bullet points. Some want a paragraph with personality.

## Heartbeat

Heartbeat is your background assistant. Twice a day on weekdays, it sweeps your connected communication channels and surfaces what actually needs you:

- **Needs you.** Things waiting on your decision or reply.
- **Drafted.** Replies it prepared as Action Cards, ready for one-click approval.
- **Heads-up.** Things worth knowing that don't need action yet.
- **Could not check.** Anything it couldn't reach, so silence never hides a problem.

It stages at most three Action Cards per sweep. If nothing needs you, it says so in one line and gets out of your way.

**Make it yours too.** Adjust the schedule, tell it which channels matter most, and give it feedback when a nudge was noise. It remembers.
