Automations are workflows that run on a timer. Set one up, and Runner does the work at the time you choose. Every day, every week, whatever fits.

Your morning briefing can be ready before you sit down. Your follow-up tracker can run every evening. You stop doing the small, predictable work.

## Set up your first automation

Let's create a morning briefing that runs every weekday at 8am.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a conversation">
    Open Runner and start a new conversation in the workspace where your email and calendar are connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell Runner what you want">
    Describe the task and the schedule:

    *"Every weekday at 8am, check my Gmail for unread emails from the last 12 hours and check my calendar for today's meetings. Give me a briefing grouped by: urgent, needs reply, and FYI. Keep it short."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the automation">
    Runner shows you a summary before activating:
    - **Schedule:** Weekdays at 8:00 AM
    - **What it does:** Reads Gmail, checks Calendar, generates summary
    - **Where results go:** Your Runner inbox

    {/* TODO: Screenshot — the automation review card showing schedule, actions, and output */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Confirm, and Runner starts running it at the next scheduled time. New automations start paused so you can review them first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your computer is asleep when an automation is scheduled to run, Runner catches up and runs it as soon as you're back.
</Tip>

## Automation ideas to try

### For your inbox

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Morning email digest">
    *"Every weekday at 8am, summarize my unread emails. Group them by priority: urgent, needs reply, and FYI. Highlight anything from my manager or direct reports."*
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Follow-up tracker">
    *"Every evening at 5pm, check my sent emails from the last 48 hours. Flag any that haven't gotten a reply. List the recipient, subject, and how long it's been."*
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Weekend newsletter digest">
    *"Every Saturday morning, find all newsletter emails from the past week. Summarize the highlights from each one in a single digest."*
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### For your calendar

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Daily meeting prep">
    *"Every weekday at 7:30am, check my calendar for today's meetings. For each one, pull any relevant email threads or docs. Give me prep notes so I walk in ready."*
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="End-of-day recap">
    *"Every weekday at 5pm, summarize my day: meetings attended, emails sent, and tasks completed. Format it as a quick journal entry."*
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### For your team

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Standup prep">
    *"Every weekday at 9am, check Linear for what I completed yesterday and what's assigned to me today. Format it as a standup update I can paste into Slack."*
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Weekly summary">
    *"Every Friday at 4pm, compile a weekly summary: emails sent and received, meetings attended, tasks completed, and Slack threads I was mentioned in."*
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Manage your automations

### See what's running

Open the automations panel in your workspace to view all your active automations. Each one shows its schedule, when it last ran, and its status.

### Pause or edit

Pause an automation anytime without deleting it. To change what it does or when it runs, edit it directly.

### Delete

Remove an automation when you no longer need it. Future runs stop immediately.

---

## Tips for good automations

- **Be specific about timing.** "Every morning" is vague. "Every weekday at 8am" is clear.
- **Describe the format you want.** "Give me a bullet list grouped by priority" gets better results than "summarize my emails."
- **Start with one.** Try a morning briefing for a week. Once you trust the rhythm, add more.
