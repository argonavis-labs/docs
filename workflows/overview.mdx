title: "Workflows"
description: "Save instructions once, then invoke them with /slug in any session or Runner."
---

A Workflow saves instructions you want to reuse with `/slug`. It's a playbook, not an AI employee.

Say you're always asking for the same inbox triage: archive newsletters, treat investors as VIPs, and keep drafts under three sentences. A Workflow captures that procedure once.

Type `/inbox-triage` and Runner loads those instructions in that session.

<Note>
  Writes follow your [tool permissions](/connections/tool-permissions). **Ask** stages an **Action Card** for review. **Allow** acts without asking. **Off** blocks the tool unless a user-created Runner has its own override.
</Note>

<Note>
  Coming from Runner Desktop? Workflows are what Skills became. Your synced Skills are already in your [Workflow Library](https://app.runner.now/app/workflows). [Migration details →](/legacy/runner-desktop)
</Note>

## Create a Workflow

Two ways, same as Runners: fill out the form, or have Runner build it with you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Workflow Library">
    Find **Workflows** in your sidebar. Click **New workflow**, or choose **Create a workflow** to have Runner interview you and write it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the instructions">
    Name it (the name becomes the slug: "Listing Email" becomes `/listing-email`) and write the step-by-step guidance Runner should follow. Be as opinionated as you are in real life. That's the point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invoke it anywhere">
    Type `/listing-email` in any session, on any device. Add context right in the message: *"/listing-email for the house on Maple, emphasize the backyard."*
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Just did something in a session you'll want again? Say *"turn this into a workflow."* Runner extracts the procedure from the conversation and saves it.
</Tip>

## Workflows and Runners work together

A Runner owns the job. A Workflow says how, the schedule says when, and the Project supplies background.

A Runner assigned the Friday review can invoke `/weekly-review` at 4pm, using those instructions and the background from its Project.

| You want | Use |
|---|---|
| "Do this the way I like, whenever I ask" | A Workflow |
| "Do this every morning without me asking" | A Runner |
| "Do this the way I like, every morning, without me asking" | A Runner that invokes your Workflow |

## Workflow ideas

| Workflow | What's inside |
|---|---|
| `/inbox-triage` | Your triage rules: what gets archived, who's VIP, how drafts should sound |
| `/meeting-prep` | Your prep format: attendees, history, open commitments, talking points |
| `/listing-email` | Your listing email structure, tone, and the details you always include |
| `/review-pr` | Review a pull request the way you like, with your checklist |
| `/weekly-report` | Your report template and where to pull the numbers from |
