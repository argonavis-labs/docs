## Runner doesn't suggest. It finishes.

You can ask ChatGPT to write you an email. But it can't check your calendar before suggesting times, look up the email thread for context, or send the reply when you're done.

Runner can. It's connected to your actual apps, so it doesn't just think about your work. It does your work.

And now it doesn't stop when you do.

**Always on.** Runner runs on a dedicated virtual computer in the cloud, 24/7. Send a task and close your laptop. Dictate a voice note before boarding a flight. The work is done when you land.

**Everywhere you are.** Phone, iPad, laptop, desktop. Any browser. Your sessions sync across all of them, and they can talk to each other. No more copying context from one chat to another.

**Runners.** Always-on automations with their own schedules and their own memory. Run one at 5am and your morning brief is in your inbox before you wake up.

**Every major model.** Claude, OpenAI, Grok, Gemini, and open-source models like Kimi K3 and GLM 5.2. Pick one, or let Auto intelligence pick for the task.

**You hold the keys.** Every connected app has per-tool controls: on, off, or "ask first." Runner only does what you've approved it to do.
