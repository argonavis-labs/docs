Runner is a desktop workspace for AI-assisted work. You can keep conversations, files, connected apps, and your own MCP servers in one place.

<Note>
  Open Runner at [runner.now](https://runner.now).
</Note>

## What Runner gives you

- Keep separate workspaces for different projects, clients, or teams.
- Bring in managed apps, local files, and custom MCP servers.
- Work in one desktop app instead of stitching tools together by hand.
